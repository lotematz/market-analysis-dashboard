Project Description:
this project is a strategic decision-making tool designed for entrepreneurs and business owners. It automates the process of market research by performing a real-timeThis application 
is a "Market Research in a Click" tool.
The tool focuses on objective data points—ratings, review volume, and price levels—to help users identify market trends and understand how competitors are positioned.
Key Features:
Instant Market Overview: A "one-click" research report for any city and business category.
Competitor Mapping: Geographic visualization of where competitors are clustered.
Data-Driven KPIs: Calculates average market rating and density within a specific radius.
Market Leaders: Identifying the "Top 5" established players with high authority (over 100 reviews).
